# UPI receipt build

Install the APK as an update without uninstalling the existing app.

1. Open **Owner → Settings → UPI Payment**.
2. Enter the verified Merchant UPI ID and Payee Name, then save.
3. Print a ₹1 UPI bill and scan it to confirm the merchant and amount.

UPI receipts include the saved final bill amount. Cash and Card receipts do not include a QR code. QR generation does not confirm payment; verify payment independently.
